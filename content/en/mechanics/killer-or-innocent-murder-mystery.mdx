### Navigating Sightlines and Chokepoints
Staying alive demands strict map awareness. Wandering into dead ends or narrow hallways leaves you completely vulnerable to ambush attacks.

*   **Avoid Clumping:** Sticking tightly to large packs makes you collateral damage during surprise slashing sprees.
*   **Establish Triangular Sightlines:** Position yourself in open rooms where you can monitor at least two exits without turning your back to blind doorways.
*   **Listen for Ambient Weapon Sounds:** Drawing a blade or firing a projectile produces subtle audio cues that reveal proximity before visual contact happens.

| Innocent Action | Risk Level | Tactical Benefit | Ideal Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Camping in Vents/Corners | High | Zero visibility, easily cornered | Never recommended against veteran killers |
| Orbiting the Detective | Medium | Immediate protection / gun access | When the detective's identity is verified |
| Constant Roaming | Low-Medium | Hard to corner, high coin/credit gain | Large, multi-floor maps |
| Camping the Dropped Gun | Extreme | Instant hero potential or instant death | When the killer is baiting from a distance |

When deciding how to handle your **killer or innocent murder mystery** round as an unarmed survivor, always treat the dropped weapon with caution. Veteran slashers frequently drop the sheriff, back away, and watch the weapon from cover to strike anyone who attempts a rescue pickup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first if I spawn as an innocent?
Immediately disperse away from the main spawn cluster. Most early-round wipes happen because players linger in the starting room where an unrevealed killer can chain multiple eliminations before anyone reacts.

### How do I pick up the fallen gun safely during a match?
Never sprint toward the fallen weapon in a straight line. Bait the killer's position first by checking nearby angles, approach using erratic jumping patterns, and only interact with the weapon if you know the killer is not actively watching the drop point.

### Can an innocent player win without ever picking up a weapon?
Yes. If the innocents successfully avoid the killer until the lobby's round timer expires, the entire innocent team wins the match by default.

### What is the biggest giveaway when determining if someone is a killer or innocent murder mystery player?
Unnatural pursuit angles. An innocent generally avoids trailing directly behind other players, whereas an assassin will mirror your directional changes to close the gap for a point-blank strike.
