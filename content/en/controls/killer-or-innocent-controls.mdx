Your objective shifts entirely depending on the identity assigned at the start of the match. Consequently, your **killer or innocent controls** prioritize different inputs during critical encounters.

### Innocent Movement and Evasion Inputs

As an Innocent or civilian role, your offensive capabilities are either nonexistent or strictly defensive. Your mechanical focus rests on situational awareness, escape maneuvers, and rapid weapon recovery if the Sheriff or protector falls.

*   **Camera Swiveling:** Innocents must constantly rotate the camera 180 degrees while running forward. On PC, holding your free-look key allows you to monitor pursuers without altering your sprint vector.
*   **Panic Pickup Buffering:** When a dropped revolver or defensive item lands on the floor, spamming your interaction key (`E` or `Square/X`) before entering the collision radius ensures you grab it the millisecond the server registers your proximity.
*   **Decoupled Footwork:** Zig-zagging by rapidly alternating strafe inputs (`A` and `D`) breaks the Killer’s direct line-of-sight and throws off thrown weapon trajectory.

### Killer Attack and Takedown Inputs

Operating as the Killer requires precision timing, hitbox awareness, and deliberate inventory concealment. One misplaced strike reveals your identity to everyone in the room.

*   **Holster Timing:** Never run with your weapon drawn. Practice tapping your hotbar slot to draw your weapon, executing your strike with `LMB`, and immediately hitting holster to conceal your blade within a sub-second window.
*   **Throw Calculations:** If your role allows thrown blades or projectile abilities, holding `RMB` activates the trajectory arc. Releasing `RMB` fires; canceling is typically achieved by tapping your weapon stow bind.
*   **Ability Activation:** Secondary active skills (such as smoke bombs, radar pings, or disguise triggers) are mapped to secondary utility keys (`Q` or `F` on PC; `RB/R1` on controllers).

The default control schemes place critical actions like stowing weapons and crouching on fingers needed for primary directional movement. Rebinding these functions provides immediate mechanical improvements.

## Platform Troubleshooting & Input Lag Solutions

Player experience reveals that input delay is the primary cause of missed stabs and delayed evasive slides. Addressing client-side bottlenecks ensures your inputs translate immediately into game action.

### Diagnosing Input Stutters and Deadzones

*   **Polling Rate Mismatches:** High-end mice featuring 4000Hz or 8000Hz polling rates can cause micro-stuttering in lighter multiplayer game engines. If your camera hitches when turning, lower your mouse polling rate in your peripheral software to 1000Hz.
*   **Controller Stick Drift:** In social deduction games, slight analog drift can interrupt stationary stealth mechanics. Increase your inner controller deadzone to roughly 5% to 8% in the options menu to prevent accidental steps.
*   **Mobile Screen Latency:** Ensure dynamic graphic scaling is enabled. Lowering shadow quality and particle density on mobile devices reduces frame drops that cause missed virtual button presses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the default killer or innocent controls on PC?
The default PC scheme uses `W/A/S/D` for movement, `Spacebar` to jump, `Left Shift` to sprint, and `E` to interact with objects or collect dropped weapons. Attacking or firing uses the `Left Mouse Button`, while aiming or preparing a thrown item is mapped to the `Right Mouse Button`. Weapons can be drawn or stowed using number keys `1` through `5`.

### How do I throw a knife or secondary weapon effectively?
To execute a ranged throw, hold down your secondary action input (`Right Mouse Button` on PC or `Left Trigger` on controller) to bring up the aiming guide. Align the target slightly ahead of the player's movement direction, then press your primary attack button (`Left Mouse Button` or `Right Trigger`) to release the throw.

### Can I play with a controller on PC?
Yes, modern social deduction and party games built on major engines support standard XInput and DirectInput controllers. Simply connect your Xbox or PlayStation controller via USB or Bluetooth before launching the client. You can adjust deadzones and axis sensitivity directly in the controls tab of the game settings.

### Why is my character not picking up dropped weapons instantly?
Pickup delays are typically caused by input buffering issues or connection latency. Instead of tapping the interact key once when standing over the item, hold the interact button (`E` or `Square/X`) as you approach the dropped gear. This queues the interaction request on the server the moment your hitbox overlaps with the item.
