## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I prioritize during a surprise killer or innocent defend confrontation?
Your immediate priority should always be creating distance rather than engaging in direct combat. Use terrain obstacles, vault points, or sudden directional changes to break the attacker's line of sight before attempting any risky parry or counter-attack.

### How does the game distinguish between a legal defense and an illegal attack?
The system monitors weapon status, proximity, and who initiated the aggression check. Striking an opponent who has already begun an attack animation is typically registered as a valid self-defense action, whereas attacking an unsuspecting or disengaged target results in aggression penalties.

### Can I win a scenario purely through passive evasion without counter-attacking?
Yes. Many advanced survival routes reward pure evasion. By avoiding direct confrontations entirely and leveraging stealth routes to gather time-stamped alibis, you can satisfy victory conditions without ever triggering a risky combat or grappling mini-game.

### Why do dialogue choices fail even when my killer or innocent defend actions were completely justified?
Dialogue failures often happen because your physical evidence contradicts minor details in your testimony, or your character's stress level exceeded manageable thresholds during questioning. Always verify your collected proof before submitting statements during formal interrogation sequences.
