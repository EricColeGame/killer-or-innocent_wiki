## Core Input Layouts: Killer vs. Innocent Configurations

Action-oriented social deduction games generally assign distinct action profiles depending on whether your assigned role possesses offensive murder tools or limited defensive countermeasures. 

When playing as a hunter, offensive bindings prioritize weapon swings, execution triggers, and stealth abilities. Conversely, defensive bystanders rely on evasion, parries, stun items, or emergency light attacks.

## Combat Mechanics: Anatomy of Role-Based Attacks

Understanding the underlying engine physics behind strikes helps you predict hitboxes and avoid wasted swings. Attack mechanics diverge drastically depending on the role assigned at the start of a match.

### Killer Attack Profiles

As the aggressor, your strikes hit harder, cover wider angles, and often inflict crippling status effects like bleeding or movement slow. However, whiffing a primary strike incurs harsh recovery frames, leaving you vulnerable to counter-stuns.

### Innocent Attack Countermeasures

Innocents rarely possess one-shot lethal capabilities. Instead, their combat profile centers on interruption, disengagement, and temporary disruption. Striking an assailant resets their sprint momentum or buys enough time to reach safe zones.

| Combat Attribute | Killer Strike | Innocent Defensive Attack | Environmental Weapon (Crowbar/Bat) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Startup Frames** | 8–12 frames | 4–6 frames | 14–18 frames |
| **Active Hitbox Window** | 0.35 seconds | 0.15 seconds | 0.45 seconds |
| **Recovery / Cooldown** | 1.8 seconds (on miss) | 0.8 seconds | 2.2 seconds |
| **Primary Effect** | High Damage / Downed State | 1.5s Stun / Flinch | Knockback / Disarm |
| **Audible Range** | Moderate (12 meters) | High (25 meters) | Very High (35 meters) |

Whenever an innocent chooses to engage in a desperate killer or innocent attack skirmish, noise discipline becomes a critical factor. Swings broadcast your exact location to anyone nearby, meaning a failed ambush exposes your identity immediately.

## Keybind Optimization for High-Pressure Engagements

Default keybinds provided by developers rarely suit competitive lobbies. Optimizing your layout reduces finger travel distance, allowing you to react smoothly during complex encounters.

```
[Default WASD Cluster] -> High strain, multiple modifier keys required
           ↓
[Optimized Thumb/Mouse Cluster]
- Movement: W, A, S, D
- Primary Attack: LMB
- Secondary Tool / Stun: MB4 (Thumb)
- Defensive Dodge / Slide: Space / Shift
- Use / Confirm: E
```

### Essential Settings Tweaks

1. **Disable Mouse Smoothing & Acceleration**: Consistent muscle memory requires 1:1 raw mouse input.
2. **Field of View (FOV) Tuning**: Expand your FOV to 95°–105° to monitor blind spots without introducing severe fisheye distortion.
3. **Toggle vs. Hold for ADS/Block**: Set defensive blocks to "Hold" for precise cancellation into a sprint or counter-swing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most reliable input method to survive a killer or innocent attack?
The most reliable method is binding defensive tools (such as stuns or parries) directly to side mouse buttons. This setup allows your primary hand fingers to stay glued to WASD movement controls, preventing awkward halts in your movement during sudden ambushes.

### Can an innocent player win a head-to-head melee fight against a killer?
In balanced social deduction games, innocents rarely win straight toe-to-toe slugfests due to lower baseline damage and shorter hitboxes. An innocent's primary objective during a killer or innocent attack situation is creating enough hit-stun or distance to alert other players and escape.

### Why do my attacks keep missing during close-quarters chases?
Missed hits typically stem from server latency, poorly timed forward lunges, or improper camera tracking. Ensure mouse acceleration is disabled, adjust your sensitivity so a single swipe covers roughly 180 degrees, and avoid swinging while your character is caught in sprinting recovery frames.
