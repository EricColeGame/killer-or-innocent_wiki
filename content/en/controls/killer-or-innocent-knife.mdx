## Core Controls and Input Bindings for Knife Combat

Navigating melee combat under pressure demands muscle memory. When an encounter suddenly turns physical, clumsy key presses will cost you the round. The baseline control configurations prioritize instantaneous reaction times across both mouse-and-keyboard setups and gamepads.

The table below breaks down the essential control schemes across major input methods, highlighting the primary actions required during knife combat.

| Action Category | Mouse & Keyboard (Default) | Controller (Xbox / PlayStation) | Tactical Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Slash** | Left Mouse Button (LMB) | Right Trigger (RT / R2) | Fast, low-damage frontal slash with short recovery |
| **Heavy / Backstab** | Right Mouse Button (RMB) | Right Bumper (RB / R1) | High-damage lethal blow, requires charging or rear angle |
| **Defensive Parry** | F or Side Mouse Button | Left Bumper (LB / L1) | Deflects incoming slashes if timed within active window |
| **Inspect / Sheathe** | H Key | D-Pad Down | Conceals weapon to avoid drawing suspicion |
| **Quick Dash / Evade** | Space + Direction | A / Cross + Direction | Rapid repositioning to avoid frontal lunge attacks |

Proper input buffering is critical. If you spam your attack inputs while recovering from a missed strike, the game queues an additional slash, leaving your hitbox exposed to counterattacks. Keeping your inputs deliberate and precise is the cornerstone of surviving any lethal engagement.

## Anatomy of a Strike: Hitboxes, Range, and Timing

Every weapon swing features three distinct phases: windup, active hitbox frames, and recovery. In social deduction scenarios, understanding these invisible boundaries reveals whether an aggressive target is wielding a true **killer or innocent knife** strike aimed to eliminate or simply flailing in defensive panic.

```
[ Windup: 120ms ] ──▶ [ Active Hitbox: 80ms ] ──▶ [ Recovery / Cooldown: 350ms ]
```

During the windup phase, the wielder raises the blade, broadcasting their intent. The active frames represent the narrow window where collision detection calculates damage. If the strike misses, the recovery window leaves the attacker completely vulnerable to counter-slashes or projectile retorts.

## Combat Frameworks: Defensive Parrying and Counter-Attacks

When forced into close quarters, standing your ground requires mastery over the parry window. Parrying disrupts an assailant's combo string, inflicting temporary stagger frames that neutralize their offensive momentum.

The table below outlines how defensive inputs interact with various incoming attacks:

| Attacker Action | Defender Response | Interaction Outcome | Frame Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light Slash** | Timed Parry (Within 100ms) | Attack deflected; attacker staggered | +240 ms (Defender advantage) |
| **Heavy Lunge** | Timed Parry | Blocked; slight knockback to both players | Neutral (0 ms) |
| **Light Slash** | Late Block / Missed Parry | Defender takes 60% chip damage | -180 ms (Attacker advantage) |
| **Heavy Lunge** | Sidestep / Diagonal Dash | Attacker misses entirely | +360 ms (Defender counter-window) |
| **Any Strike** | Jump Backpedal | Attacker whiffs; defender resets neutral | Reset to neutral spacing |

### Executing the Perfect Counter-Strike

1. **Maintain Spacing:** Keep a buffer of approximately 2.5 meters. This forces the aggressor to commit to a heavy lunge if they want to close the gap.
2. **Bait the Swing:** Step forward briefly into their effective range, then instantly buffer a backward dash.
3. **Punish the Recovery:** As the assailant's weapon whiffs through empty air, advance into their recovery frames with a light slash combo.
4. **Break Line of Sight:** Do not overcommit to prolonged trades. Land your counter-hit and retreat toward visual sightlines where allies can verify your self-defense claim.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first when approached by an armed player?
Immediately increase your spacing by backpedaling toward open, well-lit areas. Draw your own weapon strictly for defensive parrying, vocalize the encounter to the lobby, and avoid turning your back, which exposes you to lethal backstab hitboxes.

### How can third-party observers determine a killer or innocent knife user?
Observers should look at movement paths and engagement history. The aggressor typically pursues relentlessly across multiple rooms, ignores spoken requests to halt, and attempts to trap targets in dead ends. Innocent defenders backpedal, keep safe distances, and immediately sheathe their weapons once the threat disengages.

### Can light slashes interrupt a heavy attack windup?
In most combat engines, light slashes do not possess enough poise damage to interrupt a fully charged heavy lunge. Attempting to trade hits against a heavy windup usually results in the defender taking catastrophic damage. Your best response against a heavy attack is a diagonal sidestep or an evasive backward dash.

### Does crouching reduce weapon sway or footstep detection during combat?
Crouching completely eliminates audible footsteps, allowing stealthy repositioning around corners. However, crouching severely limits your movement speed and evasion velocity, making you an easy target if an opponent spots your position and commits to a frontal lunge.
