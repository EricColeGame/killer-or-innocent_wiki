## Coin Economy Breakdown: Standard, Epic, and Super Tiers

In classic Murder Mystery modes, pickup items used to be flat-value items scattered randomly across hallways. Over time, tiered currency systems were introduced to dynamically accelerate match pacing. 

Instead of requiring ten individual pickups to generate a defensive shot, specialized variants grant multi-coin multipliers. This system directly alters match tempo by shortening the window of opportunity for the hunter.

| Coin Tier | Visual Color Marker | Standard Value | Typical Spawn Rate | Strategic Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Coin** | Gold / Yellow | 1 Coin | High (~75%) | Baseline route filler |
| **Epic Coin** | Blue | 3 Coins | Moderate (~18%) | High-value contest point |
| **Super Coin** | Purple | 6 Coins | Low (~7%) | Game-changing objective |

### Impact on Innocent Progression

The math behind weapon generation changes drastically depending on which coins you sweep during the opening 30 seconds of a round. 

| Target Weapon Threshold | Standard Pickups Needed | Blue (Epic) Pickups Needed | Purple (Super) Pickups Needed | Optimal Hybrid Combination |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Defensive Bow (10 Coins)** | 10 Pickups | 4 Pickups (12 Total) | 2 Pickups (12 Total) | 1 Purple + 1 Blue + 1 Gold |
| **Secondary Arrow / Bonus** | +5 Pickups | +2 Pickups | +1 Pickup | 1 Blue + 2 Gold |
| **Full Self-Defense Loadout** | 15 Pickups | 5 Pickups | 3 Pickups | 2 Purple + 1 Blue |

Collecting a single Super Coin instantly bypasses more than half of the progression grind. If an innocent player manages to secure one purple coin and two standard gold coins, they only need a single blue coin to arm themselves. This rapid power spike fundamentally alters round dynamics.

## Innocent Survival Strategy: Route Optimization and Risk Management

For innocent players, routing is the difference between surviving long enough to clutch a victory and being caught defenseless in a dead end. Mindlessly vacuuming every coin in sight leaves you vulnerable to sudden corner ambushes.

| Zone Category | Coin Density | Escape Vectors | Safety Rating | Tactical Approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Plaza / Courtyard** | Very High | 4+ Exits | Low (High Exposure) | Hit-and-run only; grab high-tier coins and leave |
| **Narrow Corridors** | Moderate | 2 Exits | Very Low (Bottleneck) | Avoid early game unless tracking an Epic drop |
| **Elevated Balconies** | Low to Moderate | 1 Exit + Jump Drop | Medium | Excellent for holding ground once armed |
| **Dead-End Rooms** | Variable | 1 Exit | Dangerous | Only enter if a Super Coin completes your weapon |

### Balancing Movement and Economy

1. **Prioritize Peripheral Loops:** Circle the outer perimeter of the arena during the opening 20 seconds. This avoids the chaotic center where opening eliminations generally occur.
2. **Track Audio Visualizers:** If your game client supports directional sound or subtitles, listen closely for footsteps before committing to an enclosed room for an Epic coin.
3. **Never Stop Moving When Collecting:** Coming to a complete stop on top of a pickup makes your hitbox a stationary target for thrown blades or sudden sprint-strikes.
4. **Distribute Resources:** Avoid bunching up with three or four other innocents along the same route. Sharing gold spawns starves your entire group, preventing anyone from obtaining a defensive bow in time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are killer or innocent coins used for in social deduction minigames?
In most deduction minigames, innocents collect coins scattered across the map to automatically unlock a defensive weapon (usually a bow and arrow) upon reaching a set threshold (typically 10 coins). For killers, monitoring these coin pickups helps predict innocent movement patterns and intercept threats before they arm themselves.

### How do Epic and Super coins differ from standard coins?
Standard coins provide a single credit toward your inventory weapon. Epic coins (often blue) grant 3 credits, while Super coins (often purple) grant 6 credits. Snagging higher-tier coins drastically cuts down the time required to purchase defensive equipment.

### Why do some players prefer delaying the spawn of high-tier coins?
Community reports and player feedback indicate that early spawns of 3-coin and 6-coin pickups allow innocents to craft weapons within the opening seconds of a round. Delaying these higher tiers by 45 to 60 seconds allows the hunter to establish map pressure before facing ranged resistance.

### Do killers receive any direct benefit from picking up killer or innocent coins?
Depending on the specific server or custom ruleset, killers picking up coins may deny those resources to innocents, charge their special mobility skills faster, or earn extra progression experience at the end of the round. Checking your specific server's feature manual clarifies exact killer bonuses.
