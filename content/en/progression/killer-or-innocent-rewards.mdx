## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What gives better payouts: killer or innocent rewards?
On a per-match basis, a clean Killer sweep provides the largest single payout. However, Innocents can queue and complete games much faster. In terms of XP-per-hour, an organized team of Innocents rushing objectives often outperforms Killers who must play methodically.

### Do I lose rewards if I die early as an Innocent?
No, as long as you remain in the lobby until the match concludes. Many games allow eliminated players to finish secondary ghost tasks, which contribute directly toward your final payout screen. Leaving prematurely forfeits your earned currency.

### Can I spend rewards earned as a Killer on Innocent cosmetics?
Yes. The in-game store uses a unified wallet. All currency earned through **killer or innocent rewards** feeds into a shared bank, allowing you to unlock skins, titles, and voice lines for any character in your roster.

### How do daily contracts affect progression?
Daily contracts offer substantial flat XP boosts that bypass standard match caps. Completing three daily tasks typically provides equivalent XP to winning four or five standard matches without bonuses.
