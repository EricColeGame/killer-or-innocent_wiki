## Best Practices for Social Deduction Customization

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do killer or innocent skins actually change hitbox sizes?
No. In standard Minecraft, Roblox, and other popular deduction games, character hitboxes remain completely uniform regardless of visual customization. However, bulky cosmetics or wide 3D outer layers can make your avatar appear larger, which may draw more visual attention from opponents.

### How do I use killer or innocent skins to gain a tactical advantage?
The primary benefit is psychological manipulation. Wearing an innocent, friendly aesthetic can delay a detective's reaction time by a split second or deter preemptive attacks. On the flip side, an intimidating killer skin can cause survivors to panic and make navigational mistakes during high-speed chases.

### What are the best color schemes for innocent skins?
Soft pastel tones—such as cream, mint green, baby blue, warm tan, and lavender—are the most effective. These colors naturally suggest harmlessness and help you blend into casual civilian crowds across most standard multiplayer maps.

### Why do some players prefer split killer or innocent skins?
Split skins offer versatility. By using an asymmetrical design—half civilian and half monster—players can turn their "innocent" side toward the room while lurking, only exposing their menacing half during an ambush or after securing an elimination.
