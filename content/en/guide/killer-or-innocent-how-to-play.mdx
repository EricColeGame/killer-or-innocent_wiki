## Mastering the Hacking Mini-Games

A central mechanic that often trips up new investigators is the virtual hacking system. To breach restricted databases, decrypt photos, or trace cell towers, you must complete tile-matching grid puzzles. For many newcomers learning **killer or innocent how to play**, these puzzle walls represent the steepest challenge.

### Step-by-Step Puzzle Breakdown

1. **Assess the Objectives:** Before making a single move, check whether the level requires clearing specific colored tiles, dropping evidence chips to the bottom, or breaking locked barriers.
2. **Prioritize the Lower Board:** Whenever possible, match tiles at the bottom of the grid to cause cascade effects that clear upper tiles automatically.
3. **Save Boosters for Obstacles:** Combine special pieces (like line clears and area bombs) rather than using them individually.
4. **Conserve Lives:** Failing a puzzle depletes an energy charge. If you find yourself down to your last turn with impossible board positioning, avoid spending premium currency recklessly.

| Puzzle Tier | Typical Objective | Average Moves | Key Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Trace (Levels 1–15)** | Clear 20–30 basic colored nodes | 25–30 | Create basic 4-in-a-row rockets to sweep rows |
| **Firewall Bypass (Levels 16–35)**| Drop data keys to bottom slots | 20–25 | Focus exclusively on vertical clearance columns |
| **Encrypted Core (Levels 36+)** | Destroy multi-layered frozen tiles | 15–22 | Combine bombs with row clearers for board wipes |

According to player experience and common store reviews on platforms like the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glaznev.innocent), puzzle difficulty ramps up significantly in later acts. If you get stuck on a difficult hacking stage, revisit lower unlocked nodes to farm coins, take advantage of daily ad-based booster rewards, or step away to let your lives regenerate naturally rather than burning real cash.

## Evidence Analysis and Crime Scene Reconstruction

Finding physical and digital proof is the cornerstone of any murder case. In games like *I am innocent* and *Sentence*, the mystery is not handed to you on a silver platter. You must thoroughly inspect every asset dropped into your virtual device.

### How to Inspect Crime Evidence

- **Zoom In on Image Backgrounds:** Clues often sit outside the focal point. Look at street signs, reflection in mirrors, and digital timestamps.
- **Cross-Reference Timelines:** Check the time an SMS was received against the time a photo was taken. Discrepancies often reveal an alibi is fake.
- **Listen to Audio Backgrounds:** Calls frequently contain ambient noises—train whistles, industrial machinery, or rain—that narrow down a hostage or suspect location.

| Clue Category | Investigation Technique | Story Impact |

| **Metadata & Timestamps** | Compare file headers with chat logs | Disproves false alibis instantly |
| **Visual Reflections** | Zooming into high-contrast reflective surfaces | Uncovers the true identity of the photographer |
| **Audio Ambiance** | Isolating low-frequency ambient sounds | Identifies suspect geographic hideouts |

When you master the art of inspecting evidence while practicing **killer or innocent how to play**, you will anticipate plot twists several chapters before they happen.
