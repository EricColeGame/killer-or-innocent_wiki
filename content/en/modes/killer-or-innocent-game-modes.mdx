## Database Hacking Mode: Conquering the Grid Puzzles

The most mechanically demanding phase of the **killer or innocent game modes** is the database hacking system. Whenever your character needs to bypass phone locks, crack cloud servers, or recover deleted surveillance, the game transitions to a grid-based puzzle board.

Many newcomers hit an early progression wall here. While the early levels feel light and casual, the mid-to-late game puzzles dramatically increase move restrictions, leading some players to hit frustrating roadblocks.

| Grid Tier | Average Board Size | Move Limit Range | Primary Obstacles | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Firewall Basics)** | 6x6 | 25–30 moves | Static blockers, simple data lines | Prioritize line clears near bottom rows |
| **Tier 2 (Encrypted Relays)** | 7x7 | 18–24 moves | Multi-hit locks, drifting firewalls | Create vertical boosters to crack columns |
| **Tier 3 (Core Mainframe)** | 8x8 | 12–16 moves | Locked nodes, shifting color traps | Save screen-clearing bombs for the final 5 moves |

### Surviving Without Spending Real Currency

Player experience strongly suggests that relying solely on microtransactions will quickly drain your wallet. To clear high-difficulty hacking stages sustainably:

- **Farm Earlier Terminals:** Replay unlocked, lower-tier nodes to generate passive coins.
- **Leverage Reward Multipliers:** Use optional ad bonuses when your move counter reaches zero on an essential story gate.
- **Map Four Moves Ahead:** Avoid making automatic three-piece matches at the top of the grid; cascading matches from the lower third generate higher combo rates and clear stubborn obstacles naturally.

## Essential Survival Strategies Across All Game Modes

Excelling in these interconnected crime-solving modes requires both patience and analytical rigor. Keep these foundational practices in mind throughout your playthrough:

1. **Maintain a Physical Crime Board:** Write down names, alleged alibis, and timeline events on paper. The sheer volume of incoming chat transcripts makes mental tracking unreliable.
2. **Conserve Puzzle Boosters for Story Gates:** Never deploy premium board-clearing items on standard levels. Reserve them exclusively for mandatory narrative unlocks.
3. **Never Accuse on First Impressions:** The true antagonist frequently poses as a cooperative resource or helpless victim early on. Look for subtle shifts in speech patterns.
4. **Inspect Every Metadata Tag:** High-resolution evidence files frequently contain timestamps that directly contradict chat transcripts provided minutes earlier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the main killer or innocent game modes?
The game centers on three primary gameplay modes: interactive texting (branching story dialogues and phone calls), database hacking (strategic tile-clearing puzzles to unlock data), and forensic investigation (inspecting photos, audio, and metadata logs for contradictions).

### Can you beat the puzzle modes without spending real money?
Yes. While community reports note that mid-game hacking puzzles feature steep difficulty spikes, you can overcome them by farming coins on completed lower levels, utilizing ad-based extra moves, and planning cascading matches along the bottom rows of the grid.

### Do dialogue choices permanently lock you into specific game modes?
Dialogue choices do not disable specific mechanics, but they directly alter which story branches, suspect logs, and forensic files become accessible. Making poor decisions can lock you out of crucial hacking sequences that contain the evidence needed for the true ending.

### How do multiple endings work in these mystery game modes?
Your ending depends on your cumulative forensic accuracy, whether you successfully cleared key hacking gates, and how you navigated high-stakes crisis chats. Outcomes range from saving all potential victims to accidentally condemning an innocent character.
