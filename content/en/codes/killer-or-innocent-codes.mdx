### The Rules of Engagement

The defining rule of this social deduction format involves friendly fire liability:
- **Innocent attacking Innocent:** The aggressor dies instantly. If an innocent player misidentifies an ally and shoots them, the script executes the attacker as a penalty for false accusations.
- **Innocent attacking Killer:** The attack damages and eliminates the killer normally.
- **Killer attacking Innocent:** Traitors can eliminate innocents freely without taking self-inflicted backlash damage.
- **Killer attacking Killer:** Killers typically cannot damage their fellow conspirators, preventing accidental friendly fire within the traitor syndicate.

## How to Import Custom Codes in the Game Client

Setting up a match takes less than a minute once you have an active alphanumeric code. Follow these straightforward steps to load your custom game preset:

```
[Main Menu] -> [Custom Games] -> [Create Game] -> [Settings] -> [Import Code Icon] -> [Paste & Confirm]
```

1. Launch your game client and select **Play**, then navigate to **Custom Games**.
2. Click the orange **Create** button in the upper right corner to generate an empty lobby.
3. Open the **Settings** menu.
4. Under the **Summary** tab on the right side, click the second icon: **Import Code** (represented by a page with an arrow).
5. Paste your chosen code into the dialogue field and confirm.
6. Return to the lobby screen, invite friends or open the lobby to the public, and click **Start**.

If a code returns an "Import Failed" error, the workshop script was likely compiled on an older engine patch. Always verify that your lobby settings reflect current community updates.

## Winning Strategies for Killers and Innocents

Succeeding in social deduction matches requires balancing patience, psychology, and mechanical execution. Here is how both sides can dominate the lobby.

### Innocent Survival Tactics
- **Form Trios, Not Crowds:** Traveling in pairs is dangerous because a killer can assassinate you in a blind corner with no witnesses. Traveling in groups of three ensures that if one turns out to be an assassin, the third player witnesses the act and provides immediate justice.
- **Watch the Stamina Usage:** Killers often consume sprint stamina prematurely when attempting to close distance. If a player approaches you while sprinting for no clear reason, superjump to high ground immediately.
- **Utilize Detective Kits:** If your preset includes Sombra or Bastion, designate a confirmed detective early. Having Sombra verify players removes the guesswork and drastically narrows down the traitor pool.

### Killer Subterfuge Strategies
- **Isolate Stragglers:** Never engage when multiple innocents share line of sight. Wait until a player splits off to search for health packs or inspect an isolated room.
- **Weaponize the False Sparkle:** If Bastion's sparkle mechanic is active, use it in front of a group early. Earning unearned trust allows you to walk behind priority targets without raising suspicion.
- **Bait the Friendly Fire Backlash:** Step in front of innocent crossfire or provoke nervous players into shooting you first without solid proof. If an innocent acts impulsively and attacks another innocent, the match script eliminates them for you.

### What happens if an innocent eliminates another innocent?
In virtually all social deduction workshops, strict anti-griefing logic is active. If an innocent player shoots and eliminates a fellow innocent, the script immediately triggers instant death on the attacker. This guarantees that innocents only shoot when they have undeniable proof of a traitor's identity.

### How many killers spawn in an average lobby?
Standard presets limit killers to a maximum of 25% of the total lobby. In an 8-player room, you will typically face 1 or 2 killers. In larger 12-player lobbies, up to 3 traitors may spawn. This ratio preserves balance, ensuring killers rely on stealth and isolation rather than brute force.

### Can killers use abilities to fake being innocent?
Yes. Certain presets allow specific heroes to mimic verification tells. For example, Bastion players can use repair mechanics to generate sparkle indicators regardless of their actual alignment. Always watch player actions and positioning rather than trusting visual effects alone.
